A. Content of the account

← Codebook overview

A1 — Detail / richness of detail

Evaluation of the amount, specificity, concreteness, or richness of information in an account. This concerns whether the court considers an account detailed or lacking in detail, either overall or in particular parts.

Boundary conditions:

  • Do not infer detail merely because the judgment itself recounts many details. The court must evaluate the level of detail.
  • Do not automatically equate detail with length (A2) or clarity (A3).
  • If the court evaluates both the level of detail and whether that level of recollection is reasonable under the circumstances, code both A1 and A15 (Memory / Recollection).

Example:

“(…) har lämnat en utförlig och relativt detaljerad berättelse (…)”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Detailed “detailed”; “statement was detailed”; “utförligt”; “detailed about experience”
Not detailed “not detailed”; “lack of detail”; “not detailed enough”; “vague”
Partially detailed “part of it was detailed”;
Partially not detailed “not detailed in parts”; “lack of detail in other places” ; “lack of detail in decisive parts”
A2 — Length / extensiveness

Evaluation of the length or extensiveness of an account as a whole or in particular parts. This concerns whether the court considers an account long, extensive, brief, or short.

Boundary condition:

Do not automatically equate length with richness of detail (A1). An account may be long without being detailed, or short while containing specific details.

Example:

“(…) har lämnat en lång berättelse (…)”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Long “long”
Short “short in the central bit”
A3 — Clarity

Evaluation of whether an account is clear, precise, or unclear. This concerns whether the person communicates their account in a manner that is understandable and sufficiently clear.

Boundary conditions:

  • Lack of detail is not automatically lack of clarity (A1). An account can be brief or lacking in detail while still being clearly expressed.
  • If the account is deemed to be connected and logical code this as A4.

Example:

“Detta gäller särskilt som hon varit mycket tydlig vid redogörelsen för de centrala delarna av händelseförloppet, dvs. vad som hände efter att hon vaknade till igen i sängen.”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Clear “clear”; “statement was clear”; “tydligt”
Not clear "otydligt”
A4 — Coherence / logical structure

Evaluation of whether an account forms a coherent, connected, and logically structured narrative. This concerns whether the account is presented in a manner that is understandable and follows a coherent sequence (sammanhängande/osammanhängande).

Boundary conditions:

  • Code A4 when the court explicitly evaluates whether an account is coherent, connected, or logically structured.
  • Do not code A4 when the court evaluates whether an account contains internal contradictions (motsägelser). Such assessments belong under A13.
  • Differences between accounts given on separate occasions belong under B1 (Consistency across statements / occasions).
  • An account may be coherent even if it contains contradictions, and vice versa. If the court explicitly evaluates both coherence and internal contradictions, code both A4 and A13.

Examples:

  • “Sekretess X har lämnat en (…) sammanhängande (…) berättelse.”
  • “Målsäganden har lämnat en osammanhängande berättelse som varit svår att följa.”
  • “Den tilltalades redogörelse har varit logiskt uppbyggd och lätt att följa.”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Coherent "sammanhängande”; “coherent”; “framstår som logiskt”
Incoherent “osammanhängande”; “inte lätt att följa”
Partially coherent “sammanhängande i några delar”
Partially incoherent "lite osammanhängande, men inte viktikgt för bedömningen”
A5 — Vividness / liveliness

Evaluation of whether an account has a vivid and/or lively quality. This concerns whether the court considers the account to be presented in a vivid, lively, or engaging manner, or notes the absence of these characteristics.

Boundary condition:

Do not infer vividness merely because an account is detailed (A1). The court must evaluate the vivid or lively quality of the account.

Example:

“Hon har gett en levande beskrivning av händelsen.”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Lively “lively”
Not lively “not lively in how he described her”
A6 — Plausibility / realism

Evaluation of whether an account or the described course of events appears plausible, reasonable, realistic, probable, possible, or the opposite (rimlig/orimlig, sannolik/osannolik, trovärdigt/otrovärdigt händelseförlopp).

This concerns the court’s assessment of whether the account or described events could reasonably have occurred as described.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code A6 when the court explicitly evaluates whether an account or described course of events is plausible, reasonable, realistic, or probable.
  • Explicit mentions of difficult-to-explain elements belong to A9.
  • If the court explicitly evaluates both the plausibility of an account and the presence of difficult-to-explain elements, code both A6 and A9.

Examples:

  • “Den tilltalades uppgifter om vad som hänt under kvällen är i sig inte orimliga.”
  • “Tingsrätten anser att målsägandens beskrivning av händelseförloppet framstår som rimlig.”
  • “Den tilltalades förklaring till händelseförloppet framstår som osannolik.”
  • “Berättelsen framstår som verklighetsfrämmande”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Plausible “plausible”, “rimlig”; “inte orimlig”
Implausible “not plausible”; “ingen rimlig förklaring”; “orimlig”; “osannolik”
Partially plausible “somewhat plausible”; “more plausible”
Partially implausible "inte helt rimlig i några delar”
A7 — Errors / demonstrable incorrectness

Evaluation of whether an account contains demonstrable factual errors or inaccuracies, or whether such errors are explicitly considered absent.

This code applies only when the court specifically identifies information in an account as factually incorrect or explicitly evaluates the presence or absence of demonstrable errors.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code A7 only when the court explicitly identifies a factual error in an account or explicitly states that the account contains no demonstrable factual errors.
  • Do not code A7 merely because the court considers an account implausible, unreasonable, or unlikely. Such assessments belong under A6 (Plausibility / realism).
  • Do not code A7 merely because an account is contradicted or unsupported by other evidence. Such assessments belong under B3 (Consistency / support from other evidence). A7 requires an additional, explicit finding of factual incorrectness.
  • Do not code A7 merely because the court considers an account fabricated, deliberately misleading, or an efterhandskonstruktion. Such assessments belong under C2 (Fabrication / deliberate distortion).
  • If the court explicitly evaluates both demonstrable factual incorrectness and another criterion, code both.

Examples:

  • “Berättelsen verkar inte innehålla några påvisbara felaktigheter.”
  • “Tingsrätten konstaterar att den tilltalades uppgift om tidpunkten för händelsen är felaktig.”
  • “Målsägandens berättelse innehåller flera faktiska felaktigheter som har kunnat fastställas.”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Present "påvisbara felaktigheter”; “faktiskt felaktiga uppgifter”; “konstaterade sakfel”
Not present "inga påvisbara felaktigheter”; “inga konstaterade sakfel”
A8 — Exaggeration

Evaluation of whether an account contains exaggerations or overstatements (överdrifter), or is explicitly described as being free from exaggeration.

This concerns whether the person exaggerates aspects of their account or describes events in an overstated manner.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code A8 when the court explicitly evaluates whether an account contains exaggerations or overstatements, or notes their absence.
  • Do not code A8 merely because the court describes an account as restrained (återhållsam) or balanced. Such assessments belong under A14 (Balanced account).
  • Do not code A8 merely because a person provides information that is unfavorable to themselves or favorable to the other party. Such assessments belong under A14.
  • If the court explicitly evaluates both exaggeration and the balanced or restrained nature of an account, code both A8 and A14.

Examples:

  • “Berättelsen (…) ger inte sken av att innehålla överdrifter.”
  • “Målsägandens berättelse har inte präglats av några överdrifter.”
  • “Den målsägande verkar har överdrivit vad som inträffade.”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Present "innehåller överdrifter”; “har överdrivit vissa delar av händelsförloppet”
Not present “no exaggerations”; “free from exaggeration”; “none”; “inga överdrifter”
A9 — Difficult-to-explain elements

Evaluation of whether an account contains particular elements or circumstances that the court explicitly considers difficult to explain, understand, or reconcile (svårförklarliga inslag, svårt att förstå), or whether such elements are absent.

This concerns specific aspects of an account that the court identifies as difficult to explain or understand, rather than a general evaluation of whether the account or described events are plausible.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code A9 when the court explicitly identifies particular elements or circumstances in an account as difficult to explain or understand.
  • Also code A9 when the court explicitly states that an account does not contain difficult-to-explain elements (inga svårförklarliga inslag).
  • Do not code A9 merely because the court considers an account unreasonable, improbable, or implausible (orimlig, osannolik). Such assessments belong under A6.
  • If the court explicitly evaluates both plausibility and difficult-to-explain elements, code both A6 and A9.

Examples:

  • “Den [berättelsen] innehåller inga motsägelser eller svårförklarliga inslag.” (Code A9 for “inga svårförklarliga inslag” and A13 for “inga motsägelser”.)
  • “Målsägandens berättelse innehåller flera svårförklarliga inslag.”
  • “Det är svårt att förstå varför den tilltalade skulle ha agerat på det sätt som han beskrivit.”
  • “För det fall målsäganden deltagit frivilligt och båda målsägandena agerat och njutit på det sätt den tilltalad beskrivit att de gjort framstår målsägandenas sinnesstämningar kort tid efter händelserna som obegripliga.”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Present “present”; “difficult to explain”; “difficult to understand”, “obegripliga”
Not present “inga svårförklarliga inslag”; “no difficult-to-explain elements”; “no such moments”
A10 — Hesitation / uncertainty

Evaluation of hesitation, doubt, or uncertainty concerning important substantive parts of an account. This concerns whether the person provides a definite account of central events or expresses uncertainty about what occurred.

Boundary condition:

Do not use A10 for nervous physical presentation or body language. Such assessments belong under D4 (Body language / nonverbal demeanour).

Example:

  • “Han har inte kunnat ge någon entydig redogörelse för vad som hände just då.”
  • “Hon var osäker på de centrala delarna av händelseförloppet.”
  • “Målsäganden verkar vara väldigt säkert på vad som hände.”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Suggestion:

Present “uncertain”; “uncertain, but irrelevant due to evidence”
Not present “free from hesitation”
A11 — Spontaneity / free narrative

Evaluation of whether an account is provided spontaneously, freely, or without prompting, including whether the person can answer questions spontaneously when asked.

This concerns whether the person can recount events or provide relevant information without extensive questioning, prompting, or assistance, as well as whether they can respond readily to questions about the events.

Examples:

  • “Målsäganden har lämnat en spontan och fri berättelse om händelseförloppet.”
  • “Den tilltalade har på ett naturligt och spontant sätt kunnat besvara de frågor som ställts till honom.”
  • “Målsägandens berättelse har inte kommit spontant, utan uppgifterna har framkommit först efter upprepade frågor.”
  • “Den tilltalade har haft svårt att besvara frågor om händelseförloppet och har endast efter omfattande följdfrågor kunnat lämna närmare uppgifter.”

Findings

Present “free narration”; “spontan”; “kunde spontant besvara frågor”; “besvarade frågorna utan svårighet”
Not present “not spontaneous”; “uppgifterna framkom först efter upprepade frågor”
A12 — Self-experienced

Evaluation of whether an account is presented in a manner suggesting that the person has personally experienced the events described.

This concerns whether the court considers the account to have the characteristics of a genuinely self-experienced narrative (självupplevd).

Boundary condition:

Do not code A12 merely because the court accepts an account as credible or reliable. The court must specifically evaluate whether the account appears self-experienced.

Example:

“Berättelsen framstår som självupplevd (…)”

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Self-experienced “Self-experienced”; “is self-experienced”; “self experienced”
Not self-experienced "verkar inte självupplevd”
A13 — Contradictions (former B1)

Evaluation of whether an account contains internal contradictions (motsägelser), meaning that different parts of the same account are inconsistent with one another.

This concerns contradictions within a single account, rather than differences between accounts given on separate occasions.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code A13 when the court explicitly evaluates the presence or absence of internal contradictions within an account.
  • Do not code A13 merely because the court describes an account as coherent or incoherent (sammanhängande/osammanhängande). Such assessments belong under A4.
  • Differences between accounts given by the same person on separate occasions belong under B1 (Consistency across statements / occasions).
  • Differences between accounts given by different people belong under B2 (Between-person consistency).
  • If the court explicitly evaluates both coherence and internal contradictions, code both A4 and A13.

Examples:

  • “Redogörelsen innehåller motsägelser.”
  • “Målsägandens berättelse har inte innehållit några motsägelser.”
  • “Den tilltalade har lämnat motstridiga uppgifter om händelseförloppet.”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Contradictions "innehåller motsägelser”; “motstridiga uppgifter inom samma berättelse”
No contradictions “ingen motsägelser”; “berättelsen innehåller inte några motsägelser”
A14 — Balanced account

Evaluation of whether an account is presented in a balanced or restrained manner (balanserad/återhållsam), including whether the person provides information that is unfavorable to themselves or favorable to the other party.

This concerns whether the account is presented in a manner that does not exclusively favor the person’s own position.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code A14 when the court explicitly describes an account as balanced or restrained (balanserad, återhållsam).
  • Code A14 when the court explicitly evaluates the fact that a person provides information that could reflect negatively on themselves or weaken their own position.
  • Code A14 when the court explicitly evaluates the fact that a person provides information favorable to the other party.
  • Do not code A14 merely because an account contains no exaggerations (inga överdrifter). Such assessments belong under A8 (Exaggeration).
  • Do not infer that an account is balanced simply because the person mentions both positive and negative circumstances. The court must explicitly evaluate this characteristic as relevant to credibility or reliability.
  • If the court explicitly evaluates both exaggeration and the balanced or restrained nature of an account, code both A8 and A14.

Examples:

  • “Målsäganden har lämnat en återhållsam berättelse om händelseförloppet.”
  • “Målsäganden har även lämnat uppgifter som är till fördel för den tilltalade, vilket stärker tilltron till hennes berättelse.”
  • “Den tilltalade har också berättat om omständigheter som är till nackdel för honom själv, vilket talar för att hans uppgifter är tillförlitliga.”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Balanced “återhållsam berättelse”; uppgifter som är till fördel för den tilltalade”
Not balanced “ensidig framställning”; “inte återhållsam”
A15 — Memory/Recollection

Evaluation of a person’s ability or inability to remember particular events or information, including whether their recollection or lack of recollection is considered reasonable, understandable, or expected under the circumstances.

This also includes assessments in which the court explicitly considers how a person’s memory or lack of memory affects the possibility of evaluating their account.

Relevant circumstances may include the passage of time, intoxication, or other factors affecting recollection.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code A15 when the court explicitly evaluates whether a person could reasonably be expected to remember or forget particular events or information.
  • Code A15 when the court evaluates whether the amount or specificity of information recalled is reasonable given the circumstances, including the passage of time.
  • Code A15 when the court explicitly states that an account cannot be evaluated, or can only be evaluated to a limited extent, because the person cannot remember relevant events.
  • Do not code A15 merely because a person states that they cannot remember something. The court must explicitly evaluate the recollection or its implications for assessing the account.
  • If the court explicitly evaluates both the level of detail and whether that level of recollection is reasonable, code both A1 and A15.

Examples:

  • “Det framstår som anmärkningsvärt att den tilltalade inte minns en så central del av händelseförloppet.”
  • “Med hänsyn till den tid som förflutit är det naturligt att målsäganden inte längre minns alla detaljer.”
  • “Eftersom den tilltalade uppgett att han inte minns något av händelsen saknas förutsättningar att bedöma tillförlitligheten i hans uppgifter om händelseförloppet.”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the proposed response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Recollection as expected "naturligt att inte minnas alla detaljer”; “minnesbilden är rimlig med hänsyn till den tid som gått”
Recollection not as expected "anmärkningsvärt att inte minnas”; “orlimligt med hänsyn till den tid som gått”
Lack of memory “saknas förutsättningat att bedöma uppgifterna eftersom den tilltalade inte minns händelsen”, “uppgifterna kan endast bedömas i de deler som personen minns”

Clarification for Findings:

  • Recollection as expected / Recollection not as expected: Use when the court evaluates whether remembering or forgetting particular information is reasonable or expected under the circumstances.
  • Lack of memory: Use when the court explicitly considers how missing recollection limits or prevents an assessment of the person’s account, without necessarily evaluating whether the memory loss itself is reasonable.
  • If the court explicitly makes both an assessment of whether the recollection is expected and an assessment of how memory loss affects the evaluation of the account, record the code twice, including each of the findings.
