A. Content of the account

A1 — Detail / richness of detail

Evaluation of the amount, specificity, concreteness, or richness of information in an account. This concerns whether the court considers an account detailed or lacking in detail, either overall or in particular parts.

Boundary conditions:

  • Do not infer detail merely because the judgment itself recounts many details. The court must evaluate the level of detail.

  • Do not automatically equate detail with length (A2) or clarity (A3).

  • If the court evaluates both the level of detail and whether that level of recollection is reasonable under the circumstances, code both A1 and A15 (Memory / Recollection).

Example:

"(...) har lämnat en utförlig och relativt detaljerad berättelse (...)"

A2 — Length / extensiveness

Evaluation of the length or extensiveness of an account as a whole or in particular parts. This concerns whether the court considers an account long, extensive, brief, or short.

Boundary condition:

Do not automatically equate length with richness of detail (A1). An account may be long without being detailed, or short while containing specific details.

Example:

"(...) har lämnat en lång berättelse (...)"

Partially short "kortfattad i vissa delar"; "särskilt kortfattad om vissa omständigheter"

A3 — Clarity

Evaluation of whether an account is clear, precise, or unclear. This concerns whether the person communicates their account in a manner that is understandable and sufficiently clear.

Boundary conditions:

  • Lack of detail is not automatically lack of clarity (A1). An account can be brief or lacking in detail while still being clearly expressed.

  • If the account is deemed to be/not to be connected and logical code this as A4.

Example:

"Detta gäller särskilt som hon varit mycket tydlig vid redogörelsen för de centrala delarna av händelseförloppet, dvs. vad som hände efter att hon vaknade till igen i sängen."

Partially not clear "otydlig i vissa delar"; "svårt att förstå vissa delar"

A4 — Coherence / logical structure

Evaluation of whether an account forms a coherent, connected, and logically structured narrative. This concerns whether the account is presented in a manner that is understandable and follows a coherent sequence (sammanhängande/osammanhängande).

Boundary conditions:

  • Code A4 when the court explicitly evaluates whether an account is coherent, connected, or logically structured.

  • Do not code A4 when the court evaluates whether an account contains internal contradictions (motsägelser). Such assessments belong under A13.

  • Differences between accounts given on separate occasions belong under B1 (Consistency across statements / occasions).

  • An account may be coherent even if it contains contradictions, and vice versa. If the court explicitly evaluates both coherence and internal contradictions, code both A4 and A13.

Examples:

  • "Sekretess X har lämnat en (...) sammanhängande (...) berättelse."

  • "Målsäganden har lämnat en osammanhängande berättelse som varit svår att följa."

  • "Den tilltalades redogörelse har varit logiskt uppbyggd och lätt att följa."

A5 — Vividness / liveliness

Evaluation of whether an account has a vivid and/or lively quality. This concerns whether the court considers the account to be presented in a vivid, lively, or engaging manner, or notes the absence of these characteristics.

Boundary condition:

Do not infer vividness merely because an account is detailed (A1). The court must evaluate the vivid or lively quality of the account.

Example:

"Hon har gett en levande beskrivning av händelsen."

Finding Examples / guidance
Lively "levande beskrivning"

Partially not lively "mindre levande i vissa delar"; "saknar livfullhet i vissa avsnitt"

A6 — Plausibility / realism

Evaluation of whether an account or the described course of events appears plausible, reasonable, realistic, probable, possible, or the opposite (rimlig/orimlig, sannolik/osannolik, trovärdigt/otrovärdigt händelseförlopp).

This concerns the court's assessment of whether the account or described events could reasonably have occurred as described.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code A6 when the court explicitly evaluates whether an account or described course of events is plausible, reasonable, realistic, or probable.

  • Explicit mentions of difficult-to-explain elements belong to A9.

  • If the court explicitly evaluates both the plausibility of an account and the presence of difficult-to-explain elements, code both A6 and A9.

Examples:

  • "Den tilltalades uppgifter om vad som hänt under kvällen är i sig inte orimliga."

  • "Tingsrätten anser att målsägandens beskrivning av händelseförloppet framstår som rimlig."

  • "Den tilltalades förklaring till händelseförloppet framstår som osannolik."

  • "Berättelsen framstår som verklighetsfrämmande"

A6 — Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Finding Examples / guidance
Plausible "rimlig"; "inte orimlig"
Implausible "orimlig"; "osannolik"; "ingen rimlig förklaring"
Partially plausible "delvis rimlig"; "rimlig i vissa delar"
Partially implausible "inte helt rimlig i vissa delar"

A7 — Errors / demonstrable incorrectness

Evaluation of whether the court explicitly identifies demonstrable factual errors in an account or explicitly finds that no such errors are present.

Boundary conditions:

  • Implausibility alone is A6; contradiction by other evidence alone is B3. A7 requires an additional explicit finding of factual incorrectness.
  • Deliberate fabrication is C2, not automatically A7. Code both only if factual incorrectness is also expressly evaluated.

Examples:

  • "Tingsrätten konstaterar att den tilltalades uppgift om tidpunkten för händelsen är felaktig."
  • "Berättelsen verkar inte innehålla några påvisbara felaktigheter."

A7 — Findings

Finding Examples / guidance
Present "påvisbara felaktigheter"; "konstaterade sakfel"
Not present "inga påvisbara felaktigheter"; "inga konstaterade sakfel"

A8 — Exaggeration

Evaluation of whether an account contains exaggerations or overstatements (överdrifter), or is explicitly described as being free from exaggeration.

This concerns whether the person exaggerates aspects of their account or describes events in an overstated manner.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code A8 when the court explicitly evaluates whether an account contains exaggerations or overstatements, or notes their absence.

  • Do not code A8 merely because the court describes an account as restrained (återhållsam) or balanced. Such assessments belong under A14 (Balanced account).

  • Do not code A8 merely because a person provides information that is unfavorable to themselves or favorable to the other party. Such assessments belong under A14.

  • If the court explicitly evaluates both exaggeration and the balanced or restrained nature of an account, code both A8 and A14.

Examples:

  • "Berättelsen (...) ger inte sken av att innehålla överdrifter."

  • "Målsägandens berättelse har inte präglats av några överdrifter."

  • "Den målsägande verkar har överdrivit vad som inträffade."

Finding Examples / guidance

A9 — Difficult-to-explain elements

Evaluation of particular elements of an account that the court explicitly considers difficult to explain, understand, or reconcile (svårförklarliga inslag), including explicit statements that such elements are absent.

Boundary conditions:

  • A9 versus A6: A9 concerns identified difficult-to-explain elements; A6 concerns general plausibility or realism. Code both only when both considerations are evaluated.
  • Do not treat every statement that an alleged event or behavior seems improbable or hard to believe as A9.

Examples:

  • "Den [berättelsen] innehåller inga motsägelser eller svårförklarliga inslag." (Code A9: Not present; A13: no contradictions.)
  • "Målsägandens berättelse innehåller flera svårförklarliga inslag." (Code A9: Present.)

A9 — Findings

Finding Examples / guidance
Present "svårförklarliga inslag"; "svårt att förstå"; "obegripliga inslag"
Not present "inga svårförklarliga inslag"

A10 — Hesitation / uncertainty

Evaluation of hesitation, doubt, or uncertainty concerning parts of an account. This concerns whether the person provides a definite account of central events or expresses uncertainty about what occurred.

Boundary condition:

Do not use A10 for nervous physical presentation or body language. Such assessments belong under D4 (Body language / nonverbal demeanor).

Example:

  • "Han har inte kunnat ge någon entydig redogörelse för vad som hände just då."

  • "Hon var osäker på de centrala delarna av händelseförloppet."

  • "Målsäganden verkar vara väldigt säkert på vad som hände."

Not present "free from hesitation"

A11 — Spontaneity / free narrative

Evaluation of whether an account is provided spontaneously, freely, or without prompting, including whether the person can answer questions spontaneously when asked.

This concerns whether the person can recount events or provide relevant information without extensive questioning, prompting, or assistance, as well as whether they can respond readily to questions about the events.

Examples:

  • "Målsäganden har lämnat en spontan och fri berättelse om händelseförloppet."

  • "Den tilltalade har på ett naturligt och spontant sätt kunnat besvara de frågor som ställts till honom."

  • "Målsägandens berättelse har inte kommit spontant, utan uppgifterna har framkommit först efter upprepade frågor."

  • "Den tilltalade har haft svårt att besvara frågor om händelseförloppet och har endast efter omfattande följdfrågor kunnat lämna närmare uppgifter."

Finding Examples / guidance
Present "fri berättelse"; "spontan"; "kunde spontant besvara frågor"
Not present "inte spontan"; "uppgifterna framkom först efter upprepade frågor"

A12 — Self-experienced

Evaluation of whether an account is presented in a manner suggesting that the person has personally experienced the events described.

This concerns whether the court considers the account to have the characteristics of a genuinely self-experienced narrative (självupplevd).

Boundary condition:

Do not code A12 merely because the court accepts an account as credible or reliable. The court must specifically evaluate whether the account appears self-experienced.

Example:

"Berättelsen framstår som självupplevd (...)"

Not self-experienced "verkar inte självupplevd"

A13 — Contradictions

Evaluation of whether an account contains internal contradictions (motsägelser), meaning that different parts of the same account are inconsistent with one another.

This concerns contradictions within a single account, rather than differences between accounts given on separate occasions.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code A13 when the court explicitly evaluates the presence or absence of internal contradictions within an account.

  • Do not code A13 merely because the court describes an account as coherent or incoherent (sammanhängande/osammanhängande). Such assessments belong under A4.

  • Differences between accounts given by the same person on separate occasions belong under B1 (Consistency across statements / occasions).

  • Differences between accounts given by different people belong under B2 (Between-person consistency).

  • If the court explicitly evaluates both coherence and internal contradictions, code both A4 and A13.

Examples:

  • "Redogörelsen innehåller motsägelser."

  • "Målsägandens berättelse har inte innehållit några motsägelser."

  • "Den tilltalade har lämnat motstridiga uppgifter om händelseförloppet."

A13 — Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Finding Examples / guidance
Present "innehåller motsägelser"; "motstridiga uppgifter inom samma berättelse"
Not present "inga motsägelser"; "berättelsen innehåller inte några motsägelser"

A14 — Balanced account

Evaluation of whether an account is balanced or restrained (balanserad/återhållsam), including the court's assessment of information unfavorable to the speaker or favorable to the other party.

Boundary condition:

  • A14 versus A8: Absence of exaggeration (A8) does not automatically establish balance or restraint (A14), and vice versa. Do not infer balance merely from the presence of favorable and unfavorable details without judicial evaluation.

Examples:

  • "Målsäganden har även lämnat uppgifter som är till fördel för den tilltalade, vilket stärker tilltron till hennes berättelse."
  • "Den tilltalade har också berättat om omständigheter som är till nackdel för honom själv, vilket talar för att hans uppgifter är tillförlitliga."

A14 — Findings

Finding Examples / guidance
Balanced "återhållsam berättelse"; "uppgifter till fördel för den tilltalade"
Not balanced "ensidig framställning"; "inte återhållsam"

A15 — Memory/Recollection

Evaluation of whether a person's recollection or lack of recollection is reasonable under the circumstances, or whether missing memory limits the court's ability to assess the account.

Boundary conditions:

  • Merely reporting that a person cannot remember does not suffice: the court must evaluate recollection or its implications.
  • A1 versus A15: The amount of detail is A1; whether that level of recollection is reasonable is A15. Code both when both are evaluated.
  • When the court separately assesses whether recollection is expected and how memory loss limits evaluation, record two A15 assessments, each with its appropriate Finding.

Examples:

  • "Det framstår som anmärkningsvärt att den tilltalade inte minns en så central del av händelseförloppet."
  • "Eftersom den tilltalade uppgett att han inte minns något av händelsen saknas förutsättningar att bedöma tillförlitligheten i hans uppgifter om händelseförloppet."

A15 — Findings

Finding Examples / guidance
Recollection as expected "naturligt att inte minnas alla detaljer"; "minnesbilden är rimlig med hänsyn till tiden som gått"
Recollection not as expected "anmärkningsvärt att inte minnas"; "orimligt med hänsyn till tiden som gått"
Lack of memory "uppgifterna kan inte bedömas eftersom den tilltalade inte minns händelsen"; "kan bara bedömas i de delar personen minns"

Finding distinction:

  • Recollection as expected / not as expected evaluates the reasonableness of remembering or forgetting;

  • Lack of memory records that missing recollection limits or prevents evaluation, without necessarily judging its reasonableness.
