D. Overall credibility, presentation, demeanour and affect

← Codebook overview

D1 — Unelaborated credibility assessment

The court states that a person or their account is credible, reliable, truthful, authentic, or the opposite, without articulating another credibility/reliability criterion that explains that assessment.

The defining feature is that the credibility assessment itself is expressed without an articulated underlying criterion explaining why.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code D1 when the court makes a credibility/reliability assessment without providing an identifiable criterion explaining it.
  • If the court provides an overall conclusion following an assessment involving other criteria, code D2 (Overall / concluding credibility judgment).
  • Do not code D1 merely because the court reaches a guilty or not-guilty verdict. The court must evaluate the credibility or reliability of a person’s account.

Example:

  • ”Måläganden har gjort ett trovärdigt intryck.”
  • “Det saknas anledning att ifrågasätta hennes uppgifter om att hon sover djupt och känner sig berusad när hon tar sömntabletter”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Credible “credible”; “tillförlitlig”; “statement is credible”
Not credible "inte tillförlitlig”
D2 — Overall / concluding credibility judgment

The court gives an overall credibility/reliability conclusion after assessing the person or their account through one or more other credibility/reliability criteria. The underlying criteria should still be coded separately when present.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code D2 when the court explicitly draws an overall conclusion about credibility or reliability based on its assessment of other criteria.
  • Do not code D2 merely because the court reaches a guilty or not-guilty verdict. The conclusion must concern the credibility or reliability of a person’s account.
  • When the court makes an unelaborated credibility assessment without identifiable supporting criteria, code D1 instead.
  • Code the underlying criteria separately when they are explicitly evaluated.

Example:

“(...) att målsägandens utsaga inte innehåller alla de kännetecken som kan innebära att en utsaga bedöms trovärdig”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

Credible “credible”; “trovärdigt”;
Not credible “Not credible / not enough to discredit the complainants version of events”
D3 — Emotional expression

Definition:

Evaluation of a person’s emotional expressions, emotional state, or emotional reactions as relevant to the credibility or reliability of their account.

This includes expressions of distress, crying, fear, anger, emotional restraint, numbness, or the absence of expected emotional reactions.

Emotional expressions are included regardless of when they occurred, whether during testimony or after the alleged event, and regardless of whether they were observed directly by the court or reported by witnesses.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code D3 whenever the court evaluates a person’s emotional expressions or reactions as part of a credibility/reliability assessment.
  • This includes emotional reactions observed by witnesses, such as a complainant crying or appearing distressed after the alleged event, as long as they are mentioned as part of a credibility/reliability assessment.
  • Do not code emotional reactions under E1 (Post-event behaviour). All evaluations of emotional expressions belong under D3, regardless of when they occurred.
  • Do not code D3 merely because emotional reactions are described. The court must use them as part of its credibility/reliability assessment.
  • When a witness describes emotional reactions that the court evaluates as supporting or undermining an account, code D3 rather than B3 merely because the information originates from witness testimony. If the court separately evaluates the consistency between the witness’s account and the person’s account, B3 may also apply.

Examples:

  • “Det var uppenbart att det var svårt för målsäganden att berätta om vad hon upplevt framför domstolen.”
  • “Vittnet har berättat att målsäganden var mycket upprörd och grät efter händelsen.”
  • “Målsäganden har berättat om händelsen utan att visa några känslor.”
  • “Vittnenas uppgifter om att målsäganden var märkbart skärrad efter händelsen stärker tilltron till hennes berättelse.”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

As expected “svårt att berätta”; “grätt och var upprörd efter händelsen”; “visade starka känslor vid förhöret”
Not as expected "visade inga känslor”; “verkade oförändrat efter händelsen”
D4 — Body language / nonverbal demeanour

Evaluation of a person’s nonverbal or paraverbal presentation as relevant to the credibility or reliability of their account. This includes gaze, gestures, facial expressions, bodily movements, tone of voice, nervousness, or apparent ease, when these are evaluated through physical or paraverbal presentation rather than emotional expression.

Boundary conditions:

  • Code D4 when the court evaluates a person’s body language or mannerisms as part of a credibility/reliability assessment, regardless of when they occurred.
  • Emotional expressions or states, including fear, distress, or nervousness evaluated as an emotion, belong under D3 (Emotional expression).
  • Actions following the alleged event, such as contacting the police or maintaining contact with the other party, belong under E1 (Post-event behaviour).
  • Do not code D4 merely because body language is described. It must form part of a credibility/reliability assessment.

Examples:

  • “Den tilltalade undvek ögonkontakt under förhöret.”
  • “Målsägandens lugna och naturliga kroppsspråk stärkte tilltron till hennes berättelse.”

Findings

The finding section is used to provide further details for the respective codes. Below are the response alternatives available and their respective examples.

As expected “målsägandens kroppsspråk framstod om naturligt”
Not as expected "den tilltalade undvikade ögonkontakt under förhöret”
